# Lead research workflow

1. Obtain the offer, target customer type, region, desired lead count, and qualification criteria. Ask only for missing details.
2. Research publicly available business sources. Record source URLs and the date checked.
3. Distinguish verified facts from inferred fit. Never invent contact details or mark an email verified without evidence.
4. Deduplicate by company domain and company name before adding a record.
5. Save the shortlist in leads/leads.csv. Include fit evidence, unresolved questions, and a proposed next action.
6. Prepare outreach drafts only when requested. Sending requires explicit authorization.

Statuses: researched, qualified, needs-review, rejected. Contacted and replied may only be used after evidence of those events.
